Frequently Asked Questions

See what locals say about our carpentry and construction services.

What types of carpentry and construction projects do you take on?

We handle a wide range of projects including framing, lock-up and fix, decking, pergolas, renovations, extensions, cladding, and light commercial fit-outs. We work with both homeowners and businesses to deliver reliable results tailored to your needs.

Do you offer a free on-site quote?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation on-site quotes. Our team will assess your project, answer your questions, and provide a clear written estimate with no hidden costs.

Are you insured?

Absolutely. Cavstruct Projects is fully insured, giving you peace of mind that every job is covered and carried out to a high standard of safety and professionalism.

How do you make sure projects stay on time and on budget?

We believe in transparency and planning. Before we start, we'll give you a detailed project outline, timeline, and cost estimate. Throughout the job, we maintain clear communication so there are no surprises.

Do you work on both residential and commercial carpentry?

Yes. We provide construction services for residential homes, renovations, and outdoor living, as well as light commercial fit-outs and upgrades for offices, shops, and workspaces.

Do you take on full new build projects?

We don't handle full new home builds directly, but we regularly support builders and homeowners with key stages such as framing, lock-up, fix, cladding, and finishing work. If you're planning a new build, we can work alongside your licensed builder to deliver these essential parts of the project with quality and care.
